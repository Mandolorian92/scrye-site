About

Built by people who've
lived the problem.

Decision-grade geospatial tools.

We are a small, experienced team building decision-grade geospatial tools for everyone who has to make a call from imagery.

Our work sits at the intersection of geospatial analysis and disciplined software delivery. We focus on turning imagery and elevation into clear, current answers — and on being honest about how far each answer can be trusted.

Most tools assume clean data, a reliable network, and time to think. The hardest moments offer none of the three. We build for those moments instead — and we treat honesty about uncertainty as a feature, not a footnote.

The same engine that helps one team read a changing landscape helps another judge a property or assess a site — rapid change detection and confidence-graded insight, wherever the decision is made. We work deliberately with a small, select group of partners, in compliance with applicable law.

—What guides us

Principles before features.

  • Truthful under pressure

    Confidence is a first-class output, never an afterthought.

  • Offline by default

    Designed to perform with the network as a luxury, not a requirement.

  • Standards-based

    Open formats in, open formats out. No lock-in at the edge.

  • Operator-first

    Built around the person making the call, under real-world constraints.
